A choice route is a route where the receiver does not have a predetermined destination. He runs to a depth, usually somewhere between eight and twelve yards, and then he chooses. If the defender is sitting inside, he breaks outside. If the defender is leaning outside, he breaks in. If the defender backs off, he sits down in the space in front of him.
