They are not. They are mirror images, which is the problem.

Everything an offensive lineman does is built on a dominant stance and a dominant hand. A right tackle sets with his right foot back and punches with his right hand. A left tackle does the opposite, every single time, on every single snap. It is the difference between writing with your right hand and your left. The knowledge is the same and the execution is not.
