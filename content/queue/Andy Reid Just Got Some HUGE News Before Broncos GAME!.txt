It was third and ten. Denver called a zero blitz.

Let me define that, because it is the most aggressive call in football. A zero blitz means zero deep safeties. Nobody is back there. Every eligible receiver is covered by a defender in man coverage, and every remaining defender rushes the quarterback, which means by design one rusher is unblocked. The math is simple and brutal. The defense accepts that if the quarterback completes anything, there is nobody behind it, in exchange for the near certainty that he will not have time to throw.

Ja'Quan McMillian came free and nearly sacked Mahomes. And then Mahomes floated it over the top.

Here is the key detail, and it is the thing that beats a zero blitz every single time. Denver expected Emmett Johnson to stay in and block. That is what the defense was counting on, because in a zero blitz the running back is usually your extra protector. Instead, he leaked out.
