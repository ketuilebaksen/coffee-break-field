And let me explain why the quarterback sneak is such an outlier of a football play, because if you do not know the mechanics, the statistic sounds like a coincidence.

Every other short-yardage play has a built-in delay. A handoff requires the quarterback to receive the ball, turn, and extend it, and the back to arrive, secure it, and find a crease. That is a full second, minimum, and in that second eleven defenders move. A sneak has none of it. The quarterback takes the snap and is already falling forward into a gap his center and guard have widened by six inches, with three hundred pounds of linemen behind him pushing.
