Now let me explain how waivers actually work, because this is one of those mechanisms everybody half-knows and almost nobody has had explained properly.

When a player is released, he does not immediately become a free agent. He is placed on a list, and every club gets a window, usually a day or so, to put in a claim for him. If more than one team claims him, he does not get to choose. The league awards him to whichever claiming team is highest in the order.

And the waiver order runs from the worst record to the best.

Kansas City is six and two. Which puts this team near the very back of a very long line.

Every single club with a worse record gets to claim him first. And if one of them does, the Chiefs are finished. No appeal, no trade, no negotiation. He belongs to whoever claimed him first.

If nobody claims him, he clears waivers and becomes a free agent who can sign anywhere he likes.
