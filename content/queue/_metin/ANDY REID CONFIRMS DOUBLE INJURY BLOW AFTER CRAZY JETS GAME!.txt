Let me teach you press coverage properly, because it is the most misunderstood technique in football.

In press coverage, the cornerback lines up directly in front of the receiver, within a yard, at the line of scrimmage. The goal is not to run with him. The goal is to touch him. Inside the first five yards, a defender is allowed to make contact, and a receiver who gets jammed loses the one thing his whole route depends on, which is timing.

And here is the counterintuitive part. A press cornerback's first movement is not backward. It is a short lateral kick-step, because he is trying to stay square and mirror the receiver's hips, not run away from him. The moment he turns and runs, he has lost the leverage that made pressing worth doing.

Which is why press is not a speed technique. It is a hands and hips and strength technique. Pressing is for the physical cornerback with good feet and good ball skills, because once you have disrupted the timing, the ball arrives late, and late balls are the ones that get intercepted.
