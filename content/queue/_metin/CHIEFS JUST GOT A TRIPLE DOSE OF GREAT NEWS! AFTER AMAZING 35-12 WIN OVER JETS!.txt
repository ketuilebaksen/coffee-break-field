Now, on Worthy, I need to say the thing I have been saying all season about soft tissue, because it matters and it is going to come up again.

A hamstring is not an ankle and it is not a bruise. A hamstring is the single most re-injurable piece of soft tissue in this sport. And the reason is simple mechanics: the hamstring is the muscle group that decelerates your leg at the end of a sprint stride. So the fastest men on the field are the ones under the most strain, and a receiver running routes is accelerating and decelerating thirty, forty times an afternoon.

And when you come back from one too early, you do not just re-strain it. You come back on a muscle that has scar tissue in it, which is stiffer than the tissue around it, and it tears at the boundary. That is why hamstrings turn into month-long problems when they are rushed and two-week problems when they are not.

So when Reid says he does not know how bad it is, the honest read is: nobody will know until that leg gets loaded again in two or three days.
