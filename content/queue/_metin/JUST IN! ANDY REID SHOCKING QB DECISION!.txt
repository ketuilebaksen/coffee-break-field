When an edge rusher beats a tackle, he comes around the arc, from behind and to the side. The quarterback's instinct, and his coaching, is to step up. He slides forward into the pocket, the rusher's momentum carries him past, and the throw still gets out. Edge pressure, by itself, is survivable.
