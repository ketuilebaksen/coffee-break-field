And since Reid said we have got to fix it, let me tell you what fixing a kicker actually involves, because it is less mysterious than people assume.

Kicking a football is a repeatable physical motion, which means when it goes wrong it is almost always one of three things.

The first is the plant foot. Where the non-kicking foot lands, relative to the ball, controls the entire swing path. A plant foot an inch too close and you cut across the ball and pull it. An inch too far and you lose power and leave it short. That is the most common fault in the sport and it is also the easiest to see on film.

The second is the operation in front of him, which is the part nobody blames. A kick is three people. Long snapper, holder, kicker, and the whole thing happens in about one and a quarter seconds.
