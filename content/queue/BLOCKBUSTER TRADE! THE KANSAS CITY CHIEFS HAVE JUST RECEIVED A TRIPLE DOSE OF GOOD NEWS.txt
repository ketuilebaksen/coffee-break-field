Chiefs Kingdom, before we get to the bad news, let's start with the name that could change this entire offense: Michael Wilson.

What's up everybody, welcome back to Coffee Break on the Field! Coffee in hand, because there's a real trade rumor brewing and a real problem sitting on our offensive line.

Here's where the Wilson thing stands. The Arizona Cardinals receiver is coming off a breakout thousand-yard season, and his contract talks with Arizona have stalled out. The reporting says the Cardinals tried to extend him and it hasn't landed, and there are now people around this league who expect Arizona to lose him. His market is being talked about around 53 million dollars. And when analysts started listing realistic destinations, Kansas City came up by name.

Let me be clear before anybody screenshots this: nothing is done. This is a rumor, not a transaction. But think about the fit for one second, because it's almost too clean.

Michael Wilson is a big-bodied outside receiver with reliable hands who just proved he can carry a passing game. Drop him into this offense and everything reorganizes. Rashee Rice slides into the underneath role where he's most dangerous. Xavier Worthy goes back to being a pure field-stretcher instead of a guy asked to do everything. Travis Kelce sees lighter coverage in the middle. And Patrick Mahomes finally gets what he hasn't had since Tyreek Hill left: a receiver who wins contested balls on the boundary when the play breaks down.

That is a real contributor, not a depth signing. If Brett Veach gets that done, this receiver room stops being the biggest question on the roster overnight.

But here's the problem. None of it matters if nobody blocks. Which brings us to the guy nobody can figure out.

Josh Simmons is a genuinely interesting case, because at this point we don't even know what the problem is anymore.

Is he injury prone? Is he going to keep disappearing? He's becoming unpredictable, and the questions are starting. Is this a Terron Armstead type of player? A Tyron Smith type of player? A left tackle who is very good and very talented, but just cannot stay on the field?

Because that's exactly what it's been looking like.

We spent a first-round pick on him. You want him out there. You want him protecting Patrick Mahomes. What you absolutely do not want is Mahomes coming back from a rebuilt knee and playing behind an undrafted guy at left tackle and a backup at right tackle.

And I'll say it plainly: Patrick Mahomes is coming back to a very rough situation up front. If I'm him, I want the best protection of my life in that first game. I don't want to be running for my life when I don't even know yet how well I can run anymore.

Now, one thing I will give the Chiefs. They've got Kenneth Walker. They've got Emmett Johnson. They've got Brashard Smith. They have dudes who can run the football. So it's going to have to be ground and pound. It's going to have to be run the ball, because you cannot ask Mahomes to win this game by himself if the protection isn't there.

And if Kahlil Benson goes out there and blocks his behind off? That would be beautiful to see. If Jaylon Moore does the same, if these guys actually shut down the Broncos defensive line, that would be amazing.

But let's be honest, that's a big fantasy and it takes a lot of hope. Because Denver has a top-five defense in this league, and easily a top-two defensive line.

So stack it all up. Mahomes is coming back off an ACL and an LCL injury. He's being asked to be Superman again, to carry everything himself. The receiver room is a question mark, because we still don't know how Rice looks or how Worthy looks. And we're sending him out there behind a patched-together line.

Me personally? I'm a Chiefs fan, I'm going with us every time. I think we beat the Broncos. Would I bet on it? No. Am I confident? Not really, because I don't know how those guys look on that line.

We do know the interior of that offensive line is amazing. So if we want to run it, that can work. But if Denver starts stacking the box, does Mahomes have enough time to find anybody down the field? I think he leans on quick game, underneath throws mixed with the run, and we find out in real time.

So what does this actually mean for Kansas City?

Go back to the draft. We all had injury concerns with Simmons. Scouts had concerns about him going back to college, and that's exactly why he fell as far as he did. The talent was never in question. It was always about the injuries.

And now Simmons, who I still believe is a very talented left tackle when he's playing, is looking like a guy who can't stay healthy.

I don't want to make assumptions, but I'll tell you honestly: I don't even know where this back injury came from. I've been covering training camp. I've been keeping tabs on him. I knew there was an injury. I knew it was lingering. I did not know it was this bad, to the point where he can't play at all.

And Trey Smith basically said the quiet part out loud. He said that as NFL players, sometimes you've got to play through injuries. That you're never at 100 percent, and you still find a way to go out there and play.

That felt like a shot. Fair or not, that's what it sounded like, and that's what a lot of us expected Simmons to be able to do. So I guess this is a genuinely bad injury.

To be fair, Andy Reid has said that Simmons is working his tail off to get back and block for Mahomes. The man is going crazy with the rehab. Nobody's questioning his effort.

But my thing is simple: how many times are you not going to play? Because last season when Simmons went down, it didn't look good for us either.

Here's Reid on the situation, and listen to how open-ended it is. He said, we have that in our mind, he has an issue there that he's working through. I'm not putting any time restraint on when he comes back or when he can practice or how many practices he needs. I just want to make sure he's healthy and okay to go.

That is a coach refusing to give you a date. And then Reid shared what the line looks like without him. He said, if Simmons is not able to go, then we'll be in with Kahlil Benson at that spot and we'll roll from there. We try to play the five best guys, and we make our decision on that.

And here's what makes that interesting. During the preseason, Benson's tools impressed Kansas City enough that he's considered one of this team's top linemen regardless of position. That's why Reid sounds so comfortable.

But Reid also wasn't ready to fully confirm Benson as the direct replacement on the blind side, because the rookie only played right tackle and guard in college. When they pushed him on it, Reid said, I'm not telling you that, but we're comfortable with Kahlil on either side. He's been playing the left side, so I feel comfortable with that. We'll end up playing the five best guys, and right now he's one of them. If he has to be the left tackle, then he's the left tackle and we roll with it. That's just how it goes.

So Benson's preseason wasn't spectacular on the surface. But Reid saw traits, and Reid has seen enough to trust him as a fill-in starter. And remember, this is a coach who says he doesn't like making the same mistake twice. That's part of why he likes Benson so much.

At this point we just have to see what Josh Simmons does. When he comes back, and what it looks like when he does. But we do know Patrick Mahomes is confirmed to play, and that's the thing that matters most.

Here's where I land, and it's uncomfortable.

We hyped Josh Simmons up. We thought we had our franchise left tackle. Now it looks like we don't know if we have our franchise left tackle. And now it's fair to wonder whether we have to spend a first-round pick on left tackle again next year.

We have to see how this season plays out. But right now, honestly? We've been deceived a little bit by Josh Simmons. We thought he was this and that, and it's looking like the opposite.

And none of us really know how it goes from here. All we can do is predict. All we can do is hope for the best and prepare for the worst. It's either going to be a great season, a middling season, or a bad season. That's what we know.

I'm hoping for the great one. And if Michael Wilson ends up in a Chiefs uniform, the odds of that go up a whole lot.

I still have hope in Josh Simmons' talent. But staying on the field is the first step to showing anybody your talent. That's just facts.
